The problem

Lawyers use disconnected sources and tools for due diligence, legal research and drafting applications, court claims and other legal documents. Fragmented case, document and deadline management wastes time and increases errors.

02 /

Expected solution

An intelligent platform for case management, legal analysis and document preparation in one workspace. It supports tailored contract drafting, legal risk identification, deadline tracking and execution of automatable terms through smart contracts.
