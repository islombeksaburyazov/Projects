Track / 01 — Citizen protection

CyberShield

Legal assistance for cyberfraud victims

01
01 /

The problem

People affected by telephone or online fraud may not know which authority to contact or how to prepare a report. Gathering information and evidence, identifying the recipient and drafting an application can delay access to legal protection.

02 /

Expected solution

An AI chatbot receives an account of the incident, asks clarifying questions, gathers relevant information and evidence, and prepares a draft application. Once the citizen reviews and confirms it, the system submits it to the competent authority through integrated official electronic channels and enables status tracking.
