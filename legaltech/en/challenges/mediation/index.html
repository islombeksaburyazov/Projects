Track / 04 — Dispute resolution

Digital Mediation

One platform for dispute resolution

04
01 /

The problem

Access to mediation, mediator selection, document exchange, remote negotiations and recording outcomes are not connected in a single digital system. Initial dispute analysis and preparing settlement options take considerable time.

02 /

Expected solution

A platform connects all mediation stages and generates statistics. AI assists with analysing disputes, finding relevant legal provisions and developing possible settlement terms.
