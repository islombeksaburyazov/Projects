E–Muallif
Transparent royalty calculation and distribution

The problem
Without a single electronic system for calculating, collecting and distributing copyright and related-rights royalties, rights holders find it difficult to track funds and verify payment accuracy.

Expected solution
A platform connects rights, rights holders and usage data, and digitizes royalty calculation, collection and distribution. Rights holders can track calculations and payments in a personal account and receive legal explanations from an AI assistant.
