Business Navigator
A personalized legal roadmap for business

The problem
After registering a business, entrepreneurs struggle to identify applicable requirements, necessary licences and permits, and where, when and how to submit reports. Scattered information leads to missed requirements and additional costs.

Expected solution
A digital navigator identifies obligations based on the business activity, location and other characteristics. It provides step-by-step guidance, creates a reporting and payment calendar, and sends reminders about deadlines and relevant legislative changes.
